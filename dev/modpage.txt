## {#cca166}(*What's all this, then?*)
{#b2b2b2}(Now, you might be saying, "Hey, Offy, or whatever your name is, aren't there subtitles in the game already?"
Well, most contractor VO in the game (Bain's and Locke's lines) do have subtitles, but almost all other lines in PAYDAY 2 lack captions, even though they should *really have captions.*)

This mod covers:
* Heist-specific information, eg. correct patient clues in No Mercy, alarm box countdowns in stealth
* Special enemies spotting/kill callouts
* Tactical information (flashbang beeping, cloaker charging, etc)
* Flavor (Heat Speech from First World Bank, other reactions and chatter)
This mod *does not* cover:
* Most minor "foley" type sounds that don't convey important information: things like weapon sounds, ambience, or footstep sounds.

Additionally, base-game subtitles will be shown using the subtitle system in this mod.

{#b2b2b2}(This mod adds closed captions to the game to help bridge that information gap.)
Deaf/hard of hearing/hearing-impaired gamers, and people who don['](https://youtu.be/j0m4rcx0of4?t=97)t need closed captions but perhaps just like them[,](https://dooomkitten.tumblr.com/post/626960085420326912) this is for you.

{#b2b2b2}(Captions are styled similar to that of *Left 4 Dead 2* or *Minecraft*.)

### {#ffd700}(***Features***)
!!! Click to expand
* Dialogue and SFX captions have directional indicators indicating the source of the sound.
	* Contractor dialogue, dialogue from yourself, and other "locationless" sounds do not.
* Some voice lines have variations - for these, you can choose to enable variations, or just have a generic description.
	* Eg. generic "Spotted a Taser!", or heister-specific "IT'S A MUTHAFUCKIN' TASER!"
* Voicelines are roughly sorted into categories (eg. "Heister Dialogue", "Contractor Voiceover", "Enemy Chatter", etc)
	* You can choose which categories to show
	* You can choose to show/hide irrelevant captions in certain circumstances. (eg. stealth-only, loud-only, or always)
* The font size, color, and position of the captions is customizable
* The maximum number of captions is customizable
!!!

### {#ffd700}(***Technical Limitations***)
!!! Click to expand
* Many sounds may show a variant that is not true to the exact sound variant played; this is a limitation due to the fact that (to my knowledge) one cannot use Lua to get the currently playing sound variant from Wwise, which is the sound engine that PAYDAY 2 uses. This is the reason behind choosing a random line: even if you can't see the caption for the current line variation, you may still get a bit of flavor from the variation.
	* eg. you may hear "AAAUGH, I need a Medic Bag!" but the caption might read "They got me good! Anybody got a Medic Bag to share?"
* The subtitles are not comprehensive, as all captions are transcribed by manual entry. (Yes, this takes a *long fucking time.*)
!!!

|{#b0704a}(Q:) {#eeeeee}(I'm curious- the mod mentions both subtitles and closed captions, but mostly uses the term "closed captions." What's the difference?)|
|{#70b04a}(A:) {#aaaaaa}(In some languages, there's no difference between subtitles or closed captions, but in the USA, there's a definitional difference:)|

> {#aaaaaa}("*Subtitles* assume the viewer can hear but cannot understand the language or accent, or the speech is not entirely clear, so they transcribe only dialogue and some on-screen text. *Captions* aim to describe to the deaf and hard of hearing all significant audio content — spoken dialogue and non-speech information such as the identity of speakers and, occasionally, their manner of speaking — along with any significant music or sound effects using words or symbols." (from Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_captioning ))

{#aaaaaa}(Since this mod aims to give deaf or hard of hearing players access to audio information normally reserved for hearing people, its function is more accurately described as Closed Captioning.)

	* You can disable contractor vo with the base-game setting `Options` -> `Audio` -> `Mute Contractor VO`
